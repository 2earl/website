Data processing terms

Last updated: 13 November 2026

These terms apply when an organization manages 2earl accounts at its domain (terms of service). For those accounts the organization (“you”) is the controller and 2earl (“we”, see the imprint) is the processor under Art. 28 GDPR. An owner accepts them for the organization when turning managing on. They are part of the terms of service; where the two differ about managed accounts, these terms apply.

1. What we process

  • Subject and purpose: providing 2earl accounts for the people at your managed domains: signing in, single sign-on, directory sync, organization membership, access to 2earl services and to the apps they connect, and the security log.
  • People: the people whose accounts you manage (usually your employees and contractors).
  • Data: name, email addresses, sign-in methods, second factors (stored as public keys or encrypted secrets), sessions with a shortened IP address and device name, app connections, organization role, what your directory sends, and the security log. What each 2earl service keeps is described in the privacy policy.
  • Duration: while you manage the accounts. Retention within that time is as in the privacy policy.

2. Your instructions

We process the data only on your documented instructions: these terms, the settings you choose (managing a domain, single sign-on, directory sync, required single sign-on) and what your owners, admins and directory do (deactivating, reactivating, deleting). We tell you if we believe an instruction breaks data protection law. We may process data otherwise only where EU or German law requires it, and then tell you first unless the law forbids it.

3. Confidentiality

Everyone at 2earl who can access the data is bound to confidentiality. Today that is only the operator named in the imprint.

4. Security

We take the technical and organizational measures described on our security page (Art. 32 GDPR) and keep them at least at that level. We may improve them; we don’t lower the protection they give.

5. Subprocessors

You allow us to use the subprocessors listed on the security page. We bind each one to data protection obligations at least as strict as these. We announce a new subprocessor on that page and to your owners by email at least 30 days before it starts. If you object on reasonable data protection grounds and we can’t resolve it, you can stop managing accounts before the change; nothing else changes for you.

6. Helping you

  • People’s rights: your owners and admins can see, deactivate and delete managed accounts, and each person can download their own data. We forward requests people send us about managed accounts to your owners without undue delay, and help with what the tools don’t cover.
  • We help you, as far as we can, with security, breach notifications, data protection impact assessments and consulting the supervisory authority (Art. 32 to 36 GDPR).

7. Personal data breaches

We tell your owners without undue delay, and within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your managed accounts. We say what happened, what data and how many people are affected as far as we know, what we did about it, and whom to contact; we add what we learn later.

8. When it ends

When you stop managing a domain, or delete the organization, the accounts become their people’s own again and we stop processing them for you. Before that, you can delete any managed account yourself. We keep nothing for you afterwards; backups age out as described on the security page.

9. Information and audits

We give you the information needed to show that these terms are met: this page, the security page, the published source code and threat model, and answers to reasonable questions. You can audit us, or have an auditor bound to confidentiality do it, once a year or after a breach, with 30 days’ notice, during business hours, at your cost and without access to other customers’ data.

10. Transfers outside the EU

Data is stored in the EU. Our subprocessor Cloudflare, Inc. is a US company and handles requests at the location closest to the user. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. General

  • Liability is as in the terms of service.
  • German law applies; the courts of Berlin have jurisdiction where permitted.
  • We tell your owners about changes at least 30 days before they take effect. Changes required by law can take effect sooner.
  • Contact: legal@2earl.com.
