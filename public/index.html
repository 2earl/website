2earl Sign in

Decide which apps may act for you.

2earl builds open-source services around one account: you sign in once, protect it with a passkey, and approve or withdraw every app that wants to act on your behalf.

  • 2earl account

    Live

    Sign in with Google or an email code, add a passkey or authenticator app, see every connected app and disconnect any of them. Download or delete your data at any time. Stored in the EU.

    Sign in at login.2earl.com

  • MCP gateway

    Live

    Lets AI assistants such as Claude use 2earl tools for you through the Model Context Protocol, only with the access you approve in your 2earl account.

    How to add it to your assistant

  • Prompt share

    Live

    Keep your prompts and working sessions in one place, publish the ones you want to share, and use them from your AI assistant through the MCP gateway.

    Open promptshare.cc
