Prompt share: your prompts in every assistant

Prompt share keeps the prompts you reuse and the state of your conversations, so you can pick them up in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any MCP client, in a new chat, or with people you invite.

Open promptshare.cc · Connect your assistant

Prompts you write once

  • Save a prompt with parts to fill in each time, such as {{topic}} or {{tone=friendly}} with a default. Your assistant asks for them, or takes them from the conversation.
  • Pick a prompt from your assistant’s prompt menu, or just ask for it by name.
  • Every change keeps the earlier version: the newest 10 are kept, and you can go back to any of them.
  • Prompts are private until you publish one. A public prompt can be found and copied by every signed-in 2earl user, under your handle, not your name or email.

Sessions that remember where you are

A shared session keeps where a conversation stands: a brief with the goal, decisions and open questions, plus notes, decisions, questions, tasks and summaries. Load it in another chat or another assistant and continue from there.

  • Invite people with a link; they can read, or also add entries. Up to 20 members.
  • Tasks can be assigned to a member and marked done from their own assistant.
  • Download a session as Markdown at any time.

Private by default

  • Nothing is saved unless you or your assistant save it: Prompt share never reads your chat history.
  • Session text is stored encrypted.
  • Sharing, publishing and inviting happen only on the website, so text an assistant reads can’t make it share your things.
  • Download or delete everything from your 2earl account. Details are in the privacy policy.

Limits

Prompts up to 32 KB, 2,000 per account. Session entries up to 64 KB, briefs up to 16 KB, 5 MB per session, 50 sessions per account, 20 members per session.
