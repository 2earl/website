2. Visiting our websites

When you open 2earl.com or login.2earl.com, your browser sends technical data with every request: IP address, time, requested address, browser type and similar information. Our hosting provider Cloudflare processes this data to deliver the pages and to protect them against attacks and abuse.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We have a legitimate interest in a working and secure service.
  • 2earl.com sets no cookies, uses no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts.

3. The 2earl account (login.2earl.com)

Sign-in with Google

You sign in with your Google account. When you do, Google tells us your Google account ID, your email address, whether Google has verified it, and your name. We do not receive or store your Google password, your profile picture or access to any Google data.

What we store

  • Your account: Google account ID, email address, name, creation and last sign-in time.
  • Sign-in sessions: a random session identifier (stored only in hashed form), when the session was created and last used, a shortened IP address and a hash of your browser’s user agent.
  • Apps you connect: which apps you have allowed to act for you, with which permissions and since when. Access tokens are stored only in hashed form.
  • If you set up a second factor: the public key of your passkey or security key, or an encrypted authenticator-app secret, and hashed recovery codes.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. This data is needed to provide the account you sign up for.

Cookies on login.2earl.com

login.2earl.com uses only cookies that are strictly necessary for the service you request: keeping you signed in, securing the sign-in process, and remembering which apps you have approved. These do not require consent (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Security log

We record security-relevant events, such as sign-ins, approvals, revocations and account deletion, with the time, a shortened IP address and the app involved. We never record passwords, tokens or codes.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We have a legitimate interest in detecting and investigating misuse of accounts.

Invitations

While sign-up is closed, only invited Google accounts can sign in. For each invitation we keep the invited email address; at the first sign-in it is replaced by the Google account ID. Invitations are removed when they are withdrawn or when you delete your account.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We have a legitimate interest in limiting access to the service while it is not yet open to everyone.

4. Apps you connect

When you approve an app, it receives an access token for the permissions you approved and an internal user ID. It does not receive your email address or name from us. The app is responsible for its own processing; please read its privacy policy. You can disconnect an app at any time in your account.

5. Service providers and transfers

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) hosts our services as a processor under a data processing agreement. Account data is stored in Cloudflare’s EU jurisdiction. Requests are handled at the Cloudflare location closest to you, which may be outside the EU. Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Google provides the sign-in. Google processes your Google account data under its own privacy policy as an independent controller.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

6. How long we keep data

Data Kept for

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17);
  • restrict processing (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21).

In your account you can see your data, download it, disconnect apps and delete the account yourself. For anything else, write to the address in section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

8. Other information

  • We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
  • Providing data is voluntary, but without signing in you cannot use the 2earl account.
