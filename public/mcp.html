Connect your AI assistant to 2earl

The 2earl MCP gateway lets Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other assistants that speak the Model Context Protocol use 2earl services for you, with only the permissions you approve.

Add it in three steps

  1. In your assistant, add a custom connector (some call it an MCP server) with this address: https://mcp.2earl.com/mcp. In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Your assistant opens login.2earl.com. Sign in with your 2earl account and tick what it may do.
  3. Ask for what you need, in your own words. The assistant picks the right tool.

For Prompt share alone, use https://mcp.2earl.com/promptshare instead: the assistant then sees only Prompt share’s tools.

Permissions you control

Each 2earl service has its own permissions, and you approve them one by one when you connect. For Prompt share they are:

  • Read: your prompts, prompts shared with you, and public prompts.
  • Write: create, change and delete your own prompts.
  • Sessions: save to and read your shared sessions.

Making a prompt public, sharing it and inviting people are only possible on the website, never through an assistant. You see every connected assistant in your 2earl account, and disconnecting one ends its access within about a minute.

What you can ask

  • “Save this as a prompt called Code review.” Later, in any assistant: “Use my Code review prompt on this diff.”
  • “Create a session called Trip planning and write a brief.” Later, in another chat: “Load my Trip planning session. What’s still open?”
  • “Find public prompts for meeting notes and copy the best one to my prompts.”

What the gateway keeps

The gateway checks each request’s access and passes it to the service with your internal user ID and the permissions you approved, never your email address or name. It keeps a security log of which tool was used and whether it worked, not what you asked or what came back. Details are in the privacy policy.

If something doesn’t work

  • New tools don’t appear: disconnect and connect again; assistants keep the list of tools from when you connected.
  • “This app hasn’t been allowed to do this”: you didn’t tick that permission. Connect again and tick it.
  • Sign-in asks again and again: allow cookies for login.2earl.com, then try again.
