6. Prompt share

Prompt share lets you save prompts and shared sessions, use them from AI assistants, and share them. You use it on promptshare.cc or through an assistant connected to mcp.2earl.com.

  • Prompts are private unless you make them public on promptshare.cc. A public prompt is visible to every signed-in 2earl user with your handle, and they can copy it.
  • Shared sessions are private. Only people you invite can read them, and people you allow can add to them. Invite only people you trust: what they write reaches your assistant when it reads the session.
  • Assistants act on what they read. Check what yours does with prompts and entries written by others, and allow only the permissions you need.
  • Limits apply, for example on the size of prompts and sessions and the number of members. They are listed on promptshare.cc.
